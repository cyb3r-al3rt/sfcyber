What is Phishing Resilience Assessment?

We test your employees with realistic phishing campaigns—fake emails, SMS and messaging app attacks designed to look like they're from IT, HR, vendors or executives—to measure who clicks, who enters credentials and who reports suspicious messages.

Everyone who "fails" gets immediate, targeted training. Managers get a detailed report showing which teams are most at risk, and you get a clear playbook for improving security awareness across your organization.

What You Get

Simulated Campaigns

2-3 waves of realistic phishing emails, SMS or messaging attacks tailored to your industry and common attack patterns

Manager Reports

Team-level and individual risk scores, click-through rates and credential-entry statistics

Targeted Training

Short, practical micro-training delivered immediately to employees who clicked or entered data

Who This Is For

  • ✓ Companies with 10-500 employees who haven't done phishing tests before
  • ✓ Organizations handling customer data, payment info or sensitive business information
  • ✓ Teams preparing for compliance audits (ISO 27001, NESA, GDPR)
  • ✓ Businesses in India, UAE, Saudi and Europe needing ongoing security awareness

How It Works

  1. Scoping Call - We agree on which teams to test, what scenarios to use and when campaigns will run
  2. Campaign Launch - Realistic phishing emails/SMS sent to employees over 2-3 waves
  3. Tracking - We monitor who opens, clicks, enters credentials or reports the message
  4. Immediate Training - Anyone who clicked gets short, targeted micro-lessons explaining what they missed
  5. Reporting - Managers get team-level statistics and recommendations for ongoing awareness programs
