What is Dark Web Monitoring?

We search dark web marketplaces, underground forums, paste sites and breach databases for exposed company credentials, leaked customer data, stolen source code or discussions about targeting your organization.

You get a comprehensive report showing exactly what attackers can find about your business, which employee accounts are compromised, and specific actions to take to secure exposed credentials before they're used in attacks.

What We Search For

Credential Leaks

Employee email addresses and passwords exposed in breaches, sold on dark web markets or posted to paste sites

Company Data

Internal documents, source code, customer databases or intellectual property leaked or offered for sale

Brand Mentions

Discussions in hacker forums about targeting your company, planning attacks or sharing vulnerabilities

Who This Is For

  • ✓ Companies that experienced a security incident and want to know what leaked
  • ✓ Businesses handling sensitive customer or financial data
  • ✓ Organizations preparing for cyber insurance applications or renewals
  • ✓ Companies in high-risk industries (finance, healthcare, government)

How It Works

  1. Scoping - We collect company domains, email patterns, key employee names and brand terms to monitor
  2. Dark Web Search - Automated and manual searches across marketplaces, forums, paste sites and breach databases
  3. Analysis - We verify findings, assess severity and identify which credentials are actively being sold or shared
  4. Reporting - Detailed report with exposed credentials, leaked data samples and specific remediation steps
  5. Remediation Support - Guidance on credential resets, account lockdowns and preventing future exposure
